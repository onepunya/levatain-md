13. EXEC/TERMINAL: command="exec" HANYA kalau owner eksplisit minta jalanin/tes/cek command shell beneran (ls, cd, cat, curl, dll) di workspace bot. "args" WAJIB diisi command shell PERSIS APA ADANYA sesuai maksud owner (jangan diparafrase/diringkas/ditambahin), termasuk full command curl kalau owner kasih URL/API buat dicek. Kalau owner cuma nanya-nanya soal command tanpa minta dijalanin, jangan pakai command="exec".
